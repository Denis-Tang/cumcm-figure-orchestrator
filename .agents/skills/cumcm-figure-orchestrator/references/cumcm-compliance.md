# CUMCM compliance profile

Verified against the official CUMCM site on 2026-08-31. Recheck the current year's notice before submission.

## Paper packaging

- A4 paper; margins at least 2.5 cm.
- Abstract no more than one page.
- Do not include a table of contents.
- Main body no more than 30 pages.
- Electronic paper Word/PDF no more than 20 MB; PDF is recommended.
- Submit complete runnable source programs and supporting materials.

Official source: [CUMCM 论文格式规范](https://www.mcm.edu.cn/html_cn/node/4cd596519c9eb9fbd866398f6df0caa3.html)

## AI use (effective 2026-09-01)

- AI assistance is allowed, but core modeling and solution work must remain team-led.
- Manually review every AI-assisted result.
- Add an `AI工具使用声明` to the paper.
- Add `AI工具使用详情.pdf` to the support package.
- Record tool/model and version, purpose, prompts or process summary, whether output was adopted, human edits, and verification method.

Official source: [CUMCM AI 工具使用规定](https://www.mcm.edu.cn/html_cn/node/fef94648f2836ab6cc81586f4c38512b.html)

## Figure-specific application

The manifest stores disclosure fields per figure. If a Skill or model influenced chart choice, diagram structure, caption, code, styling, or verification, record it. Deterministic rendering does not erase upstream AI assistance. Keep prompts concise in the manifest and preserve the full necessary interaction detail in the support-package report.
